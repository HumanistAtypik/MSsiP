Last year, we started an ambitious project: create a database-driven version of Logseq. And we're getting close to releasing it, but we need a bit of your help.

While storing graphs as Org and Markdown files has worked well for almost 5 years, many of you need better tools to structure your notes—and a more stable experience overall. That's why earlier this year we announced work on "Logseq DB".

In the past two months, we've tested an early version of Logseq DB with a small group of diverse testers. But as the new version is getting more stable, we want to invite more dedicated users (like you) to give us feedback on what we've built so far.

We gather feedback via our Discord server and GitHub repository. To get an invite (and give us your direct feedback), please tell us your Discord username and the email address you use for Logseq Sync (we'll test Sync and Real-Time Collaboration at a later stage, that's why we're asking).

Note: We invite new testers in batches, and it's on "first come, first serve" basis. However, signing up does not guarantee you will get an invite to the alpha test (as we might be ready for a public release before inviting everyone). Once you're added to the tester group, you will receive of notification from the Logseq server on Discord (so make sure you've joined)."
